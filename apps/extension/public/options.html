How the desktop app collects, filters and checks your timeline. Saving also sends these settings to the desktop app.

Collection modes

Mode A (always available): while you browse X yourself, the extension passively keeps the timeline responses your browser already received.

Off by default, and risky. Mode B opens X in a hidden tab and scrolls it automatically until enough posts are gathered. Automated browsing sits in a grey area of X's Terms of Service and may put your account at risk (rate limits, locks or suspension). Only enable it if you understand and accept that. See the README section “Mode B and its risks”.

A run collects “Posts to collect” distinct organic posts (no ads, no repeats). It also stops after 10 minutes, after about 20 seconds without a new post, or when X shows a login, error or rate-limit page. The hidden window closes when the run ends.

Use the corner window if the minimized one stops loading posts. Browsers keep at least half of a window on screen, so the corner window stays partly visible. A background tab is not offered: X stopped loading there.

Fast scrolls twice as quickly, but may be more likely to be noticed by X. Save before collecting.

Developer capture

Off by default. While on, the HomeTimeline responses X sends your browser are forwarded verbatim to the native host, which writes them outside the repo to %LOCALAPPDATA%\nmc\captures\<session time>\ (or under NMC_INBOX_DIR if set), with a counts/times-only summary.json. These raw dumps are personal data (your own home timeline) — never share them and never commit them. For development/debugging only.

The badge shows the running count of distinct organic posts (ads and repeats not counted); you get a notification when “Posts to collect” were captured. Capture does not stop at that number. Toggling developer capture starts a new count and a new capture folder.

Reddit is captured too, into a separate …-reddit folder. Rebuild the native host (pnpm build) before opening a Reddit tab with capture on — an older host mixes Reddit data into the X folder.
