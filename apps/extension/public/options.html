Your filtered timeline ends after this many posts. No infinite scroll. This is how many posts you read — not how many are collected: the developer capture target below counts raw posts gathered before filtering and is set separately.

Collection modes

Mode A (always available): while you browse X yourself, the extension passively keeps the timeline responses your browser already received.

Off by default, and risky. Mode B opens X in a hidden tab and scrolls it automatically until enough posts are gathered. Automated browsing sits in a grey area of X's Terms of Service and may put your account at risk (rate limits, locks or suspension). Only enable it if you understand and accept that. See the README section “Mode B and its risks”.

Distinct organic posts (no ads, no repeats). A run also stops after 10 minutes, after 8 scrolls in a row without a new post, or when X shows a login, error or rate-limit page. The hidden tab closes when the run ends.

Browsers may stop loading pages nobody can see. Try each surface once and compare the last-run numbers below. Save before collecting.

Developer capture

Off by default. While on, the HomeTimeline responses X sends your browser are forwarded verbatim to the native host, which writes them outside the repo to %LOCALAPPDATA%\nmc\captures\<session time>\ (or under NMC_INBOX_DIR if set), with a counts/times-only summary.json. These raw dumps are personal data (your own home timeline) — never share them and never commit them. For development/debugging only.

Counts distinct organic posts — ads and posts repeated across responses are not counted. The badge shows the running count; you get a notification when this many were captured. Capture does not stop at the target. Toggling developer capture starts a new count and a new capture folder.
